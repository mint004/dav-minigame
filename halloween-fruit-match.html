  • Fill the whole rectangle. No need to draw rounded corners: the game clips them at about 12% of the width (60 px on a 500 px image).
  • Keep important parts inside a safe margin: 10% left and right (50 px), 10% top and bottom (70 px).
  • Cards are small on screen. Use one bold subject per card, thick lines and strong contrast.
  • The card back uses the same spec. Keep its pattern symmetric, because cards turn to face the way they walk.
  • If the ratio is not 5:7, the image is cropped to fill the card.
